Data we handle

  • Account email and Google identity fields supplied through Supabase authentication.
  • Payment/order metadata processed by Razorpay and stored in Supabase. The app must not collect full card or UPI credentials.
  • When a user checks the AI data-sharing notice and invokes AI Lab, the first 18,000 characters of extracted PDF text, or the code/tests entered, are sent to the configured AI provider. The original PDF file is not uploaded by AI Lab.
  • AI request metadata (user ID, feature, input character count, timestamp) is stored for monthly limits. Prompt/output content is not stored by this app by default. The AI provider may have separate retention terms that must be disclosed after a provider is selected.
  • PDF reading, OCR and Python tests run in the browser; third-party libraries are downloaded from public CDNs.
  • For draft recovery, code, tests and extracted PDF text may be stored in this browser’s local storage. AI output and test-run history are not included in automatic local drafts. Clearing the workspace removes the saved draft from this browser; downloaded backups are separate files and are not removed.

Purpose and providers

Data supports authentication, AI responses when requested, usage limits, payment verification, abuse prevention and support. Providers may include Supabase, Google OAuth, Cloudflare, Razorpay and a yet-to-be-selected AI provider. Review and disclose each provider before launch.

Retention, deletion and rights

An authenticated account export endpoint returns app-held order, current entitlement and AI-usage metadata (up to 1,000 orders and 5,000 usage rows). An account deletion action deletes the Supabase auth user and linked app rows after explicit confirmation. It does not automatically refund payments or delete records retained by Razorpay, Google, email, hosting/CDN or AI providers. Retention periods, grievance contact and applicable user rights must be finalized; the operator must verify provider deletion/retention behavior. Interim contact: [ADD SUPPORT EMAIL].

Security

Secrets must remain server-side. No online service can guarantee perfect security. This draft requires review for applicable Indian data-protection, consumer, tax and payment requirements.
